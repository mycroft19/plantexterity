Inside the Lab

Science you can build on

We produce scientifically validated, sustainable solutions in plant tissue culture, aquaculture, and algal biotechnology — bridging academic research and real-world application.

Every product and protocol is developed in a controlled lab environment, led by PhD-level expertise in marine biotechnology and microbiology, and managed with MBA-level expertise in finance and HR.

About the lab →

Our Facility

Four rooms, one sterile workflow

Media is prepared under laminar flow, sterilised in the autoclave, inoculated and grown in a dedicated culture room — each step in its own controlled space.
